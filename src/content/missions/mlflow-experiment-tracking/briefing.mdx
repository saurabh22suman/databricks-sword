# Mission Briefing: MLflow Experiment Tracking

## Situation Report

**Client:** QuantumRisk Analytics — a fintech company building credit scoring models for 15 million customers.

**Crisis:** Their data science team runs experiments across notebooks with no tracking system. Models are promoted via Slack messages. Last month, a model trained on stale data was deployed, causing $2.3M in bad credit decisions.

**Your Mission:** Implement MLflow-based experiment tracking and model registry to bring order, reproducibility, and governance to their ML workflow.

## Objectives
- Map the MLflow registry architecture: experiments, runs, and models
- Build the experiment workflow from data prep through model registration
- Implement tracking code: log params, metrics, artifacts, and register models
- Validate mastery of MLflow concepts

## Key Intelligence
- **Experiment Tracking:** Groups ML runs; each run records parameters, metrics, artifacts, and tags
- **Model Registry:** Centralized hub for model lifecycle with versions, aliases (Production, Staging, Champion, Challenger), and lineage
- **Why it matters:** Without tracking, no one knows which hyperparameters produced the best model; models can't be reproduced; regulatory auditors can't trace predictions back to source
