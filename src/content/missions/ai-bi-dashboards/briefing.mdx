## What are AI/BI Dashboards?

Lakeview dashboarding augmented with AI features:

- **Genie Panel** — embed Genie Space for natural-language Q&A
- **Dataset Relationships** — semantic joins between datasets
- **AI Visualizations** — "show me X by Y" auto-generates charts
- **AI Insights** — "what's interesting?" gets LLM summaries

## Why this matters

Classic dashboards answer what you built. AI/BI answers follow-ups: chart → Genie panel → AI visualization.

1. Understand architecture: datasets, Genie panel, AI insights

## Key concepts

- **Dataset** — UC table/query bound to dashboard
- **Dataset Relationship** — semantic join for cross-filtering
- **Genie Panel** — hosts Genie Space
- **AI Insight** — LLM summary of chart patterns
