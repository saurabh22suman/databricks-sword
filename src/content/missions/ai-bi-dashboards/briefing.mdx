## What are AI/BI Dashboards?

**AI/BI Dashboards** are the next generation of Databricks' native dashboarding product (Lakeview), augmented with AI features. Beyond classic Lakeview (canvas, widgets, parameters, alerts), AI/BI Dashboards add:

- **Genie Panel** — embed a Genie Space directly in the dashboard for natural-language Q&A
- **Dataset Relationships** — declare semantic joins between datasets in the dashboard; the dashboard respects them across widgets
- **AI-Generated Visualizations** — ask "show me X by Y" and the canvas generates a chart
- **AI Insights** — per-widget, ask "what's interesting in this chart?" and get an LLM-generated summary

## Why this matters

Classic dashboards answer questions you anticipated when you built them. AI/BI Dashboards answer **follow-up questions** in real time:

- "What was revenue last quarter?" → classic dashboard chart
- "Why did revenue drop in March?" → Genie panel pulls the answer from your data
- "Show me revenue by region as a bar chart" → AI-generated visualization
- "What's the most striking trend in this chart?" → AI insight

## Key concepts you'll use

- **Dataset** — a UC table or SQL query result bound to the dashboard
- **Dataset Relationship** — a semantic join declared between datasets, enabling cross-filtering
- **Genie Panel** — a widget that hosts a Genie Space inside the dashboard
- **AI Insight** — an LLM-generated text summary of a chart's most notable patterns
- **Parameter** — a dashboard variable that drives filters across widgets
- **Cross-filter** — clicking a bar in one chart filters all related charts
