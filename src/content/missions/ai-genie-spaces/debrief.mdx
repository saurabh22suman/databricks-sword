## Key takeaways

| Concept | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Trusted Asset** | Whitelists a table/view/function that Genie is allowed to query |
| **Genie Instruction** | Plain-English guidance for ambiguous business terms |
| **Metric View** | UC-native metric definition Genie prefers for "what was revenue last quarter?" |
| **Trusted Function** | A SQL/Python function Genie can call to compute on the fly |
| **Feedback Loop** | User-edited SQL becomes training signal for future answers |

## Glossary

- **Genie Space** — a configured instance of Genie with its own trusted assets, instructions, and conversation history
- **NL→SQL** — Natural Language to SQL: the translation problem Genie solves
- **Semantic Layer** — the collection of metric views, descriptions, and instructions that make Genie accurate
- **Hallucination** — when Genie invents columns or joins that don't exist; mitigated by Trusted Assets and Instructions
